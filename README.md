# 📊 Superstore Sales Dashboard (Power BI)

## 📌 Project Overview

This Power BI dashboard analyzes the Superstore Sales dataset to provide actionable business insights through interactive visualizations and KPIs.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze sales performance
- Monitor profitability
- Identify top-performing products
- Compare regional performance
- Support business decision-making
---
## 📈 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

- Total Sales
- Total Profit
- Profit Margin %
- Total Quantity
- Average Order Value

---

## 📊 Dashboard Visuals

- Monthly Sales Trend
- Category-wise Profit
- Region-wise Sales
- Sales by Segment
- Top 10 Products by Sales
- State-wise Sales Map

---

## 🎛️ Filters

- Year
- Region
- Category
- Segment

---

## 🛠️ Tools Used

- Power BI Desktop
- DAX
- Power Query
- CSV Dataset

---

## 📂 Files Included

- Superstore_Sales_Dashboard.pbix
- Superstore_Sales_Dashboard_Report.pdf
- Dashboard_Screenshot.png
- Superstore.csv

---

## 📌 Key Business Insights

- Technology category generated the highest profit.
- The South region achieved the highest sales.
- Consumer segment contributed the largest share of sales.
- A small number of products generated a significant portion of revenue.
